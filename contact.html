FAQ

Good to know

How do I book an appointment?

Tap Book an Appointment on any page. You'll pick your barber and service on our Squire booking page. You can also book with a specific barber from their profile on the team page.

Where are you and how do I get here?

We're at 1154 Morningside Ave #212, Scarborough. The TTC 116 Morningside bus stops close by, and there is street parking nearby. Check Milner Ave and the side streets.

What are your hours?

Tuesday to Saturday, 11 AM to 7 PM. We're closed Sunday and Monday.

Do you take walk-ins?

Yes, when a barber is free. Booking an appointment is preferred so your spot is held.

What payment do you accept?

Cash or card.

What do services cost?

Prices depend on the service and the barber. See the services and pricing list, or check the full menu when you book.

Do you do braids?

Yes. We offer 2 braid, 4 braid, and 6+ braid styles. You can book them online.

How do Saint Apparel orders work?

Place your order on the apparel page. Apparel is pickup only at the studio. After you order, we email you to confirm payment and pickup.
